Gallerisse Pin Planner

A private Pin scheduling tool for the GallerisseArt shop

Gallerisse Pin Planner helps the owner of GallerisseArt, a small shop selling printable digital art, share her own artwork on her own Pinterest account.

How it works

The tool prepares Pin drafts from the shop's own listings: an image, a title, a short description, a board and a link to the matching Etsy listing. The shop owner reviews every draft and approves or rejects each Pin individually. Only approved Pins are published, at a calm pace of a few Pins per day, to the owner's own boards.

Who uses it

Only the shop owner, on her own Pinterest account. The tool is not offered to other users, does not access other Pinners' data and does not run ads.
