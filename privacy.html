Gallerisse Pin Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · About the app

Gallerisse Pin Planner is a private tool used only by the owner of the GallerisseArt shop (etsy.com/shop/GallerisseArt) to schedule Pins of her own artwork on her own Pinterest account.

What the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Pinterest OAuth), the app reads the list of the owner's own boards and creates Pins that the owner has individually reviewed and approved.
  • The app does not access other Pinners' data, does not run ads and does not collect data from visitors.

What is stored

  • The Pinterest access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's protected credential store. It is never shared, sold or uploaded to any third party.
  • The app keeps a local record of the owner's own approved Pins (title, image link, board, schedule) to avoid duplicates. No personal data of other people is stored.

Sharing

No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security / Apps), after which the app can no longer act on the account.

Contact

Questions: please contact GallerisseArt via Etsy messages at etsy.com/shop/GallerisseArt.
